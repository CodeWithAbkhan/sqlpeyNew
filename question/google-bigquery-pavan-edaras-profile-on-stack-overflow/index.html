A Software Engineer on the BigQuery Team at Google - Meet Pavan Edara
Meet Pavan Edara, a talented software engineer working on Google’s BigQuery team, a team that develops a fully-managed, petabyte-scale, low-cost analytics data warehouse. In this Q&A Session, we’ll learn more about Pavan’s role, his responsibilities, and some of the projects he has worked on during his tenure at Google.

What is BigQuery?

If you’re not already familiar, BigQuery is a popular data warehousing solution used by organizations worldwide to store and analyze large datasets. BigQuery allows for real-time querying and analysis of data, which can be invaluable for businesses looking to make data-driven decisions in real-time. The platform also offers integrations with popular tools and services like Google Analytics, Data Studio, and more.

What does Pavan do on the BigQuery team?

As a software engineer on the BigQuery team, Pavan is responsible for a wide range of tasks related to the platform’s ongoing development and maintenance. These tasks may include:

  • Designing and implementing new features
  • Developing and debugging production issues
  • Writing and maintaining code documentation
  • Collaborating with other teams within Google to ensure seamless integration with other Google technologies

Essentially, Pavan is a key player in the ongoing development and maintenance of BigQuery. His contributions ensure that the platform remains reliable, efficient, and meets the evolving needs of its users.

What are some notable projects Pavan has worked on?

During his time at Google, Pavan has worked on a number of high-profile projects related to BigQuery. One such project was the development and implementation of a new feature called “batch loading,” which allows users to load large amounts of data into BigQuery in a more streamlined and efficient manner.

To accomplish this, Pavan and his team had to reverse-engineer the existing codebase to determine where optimizations could be made. The team also worked with other teams at Google to ensure that the new feature seamlessly integrated with other Google technologies, like Google Cloud Storage.

Another project that Pavan worked on was the development of a new API for BigQuery, which allows developers to programmatically interact with the platform. This was a significant undertaking, as the previous API was complex, difficult to use, and slow. Through this project, Pavan was able to make the API more user-friendly, efficient, and accessible to developers of all skill levels.

Pavan Edara is a talented software engineer with a wealth of experience working on Google’s BigQuery team.
