Google BigQuery: Personal Saved Queries Disappeared, What to Do?

Summary

Upon logging in to Google BigQuery, a user discovered that all of their saved personal queries had disappeared. The project queries were still present, but the personal queries were gone. The user needs the personal queries for their work and does not want to rewrite them from scratch. They have already tried logging in and out several times, but it did not help.

Issue Presentation

Today I logged in to BigQuery and found all of my saved personal queries disappeared. It says “No saved queries yet. Write and save a query to get started.” I had a lot of large queries that I still need and I have no desire to rewrite them from scratch. Project queries are still there, and I can perform new queries and go through query history. I tried logging out and logging in several times with zero effect. Has anyone encountered this kind of issue before?

Solution

After checking with user reports and feedback on Google Issue Tracker, it seems like a technical glitch within Google BigQuery. The user has already submitted a bug report to Google, and it is advised to wait for a response from the tech support team. Although, there are a few possible options or alternatives in the meantime.

Firstly, it is worth checking under the “Saved Queries” tab, which should be present both below and in the left panel of the BigQuery interface. There is a possibility that the personal queries got misplaced or hidden under different filters, and they can be retrieved from here.

Secondly, it is possible to use backups to restore lost queries. If the user had backed up their queries recently, they could restore them via the undelete feature. One can identify the query that wants to restore from the history and click on the “Query Information” icon, and then on “Show deleted queries” to see if it is available for restoration.

Another option is to reach out to the support team of Google BigQuery by opening a ticket on their official website. The experienced support team can provide personalized assistance and help restore lost queries or identify any underlying issues.

Updated Information

As mentioned by a user in the comments section, the “Saved Queries” tab is now available under the Explorer tab. Google has updated the interface for BigQuery, and the documentation on their website is also outdated. The new tab is accessible from the left-hand menu by selecting “Explorer,” which will open up the query editor and provide access to the saved queries.

Recap

If your saved personal queries disappear in Google BigQuery, it is essential not to panic and try to retrieve them via alternative methods. Checking under the “Saved Queries” tab, using backups to restore lost queries, and reaching out to support teams should be your first steps. Overall, it is always best practice to backup your queries, and you should immediately do so in case this issue occurs again in the future. ]]>
