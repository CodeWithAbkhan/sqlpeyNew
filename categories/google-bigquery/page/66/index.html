GOOGLE BIGQUERY: Round Timestamp to Nearest 15 Minute Intervals in BigQuery

Question: How can I round a timestamp to the nearest 15-minute interval using BigQuery standard SQL? Solution: To round a timestamp to the nearest 15-minute interval in BigQuery standard SQL, you can use the following formula: TIMESTAMP_SECONDS(900 * DIV(UNIX_SECONDS(dt_from) + 450, 900)) This formula works by converting the timestamp to Unix time (the number of seconds since January 1, 1970), adding 450 seconds (7.5 minutes) to it, dividing the result by 900 (the number of seconds in 15 minutes), rounding down to the nearest integer, and then multiplying by 900 to get the Unix time of the nearest 15-minute interval.
