What is ERD Diagram?

An Entity Relationship Diagram (ERD) is a amazing tool used to visualize your database structure and relationships between different entities. Itâ€™s essential for database design and development, as it helps you understand the underlying structure of your database, and how the different objects in the database interact with each other.

ERD or entity relationship diagram is basically a graphical representation of Database table structure, how tables and their attributes are connected with each other and what type of relationships exists between them. Creating an ERD diagram is very important step in database development process, it helps us to elaborate database structure to meet our business requirement. ERD diagram is consists of following objects.

  1. Attributes

  2. Entities / Table

  3. Relationship / Arrows

1.     Attributes

Simply an Attribute is called the property of an Entity

2.     Entities in ERD

Entity in ERD diagram is very important object, which have properties like name, attribute names, primary keys, foreign keys, simply Entity in database diagram is graphical representation of tables and their columns, constraints and keys.

3.     Relationships

Entities may have relationship between them, e.g. an person have many hobbies it means that there is one-to-many relationship between person entity and hobby entity and it is represented by arrows used to connect entities.

There are different types of relationships

  • One-to-one

  • One-to-one (optional)

  • One-to-many

  • One-to-many (optional)

  • Many-to-many

  • Many-to-many (optional)

Note: the circle in the arrow shows that it is optional, e.g. in a relationship between person entity and hobby entity can be one-to-many (optional) it means that a person can have one or many hobbies and maybe there is a person who donâ€™t have any hobby.
