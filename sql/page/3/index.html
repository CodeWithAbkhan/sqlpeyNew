What is Trigger in SQL? 2023

Trigger is a special type of stored procedure that is executed automatically in response to certain events on a database table Triggers are used to enforce business rules, maintain data integrity, and perform tasks such as auditing. Triggers can be activated by various events, such as INSERT , UPDATE , DELETE , or SELECT statements on a table, or by direct invocation using the EXECUTE or CALL statement. Triggers can also be activated by database events such as logon, logoff, or server startup.
